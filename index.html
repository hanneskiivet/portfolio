Custom Objects - Record List UX

A brief for the record list view of custom objects: an intro, every capability as a story, and two interactive mockups. Open a section to read it.

Intro

Companies often need to keep track of things that do not fit the built-in record types, such as a fleet of cars, a set of properties or a list of contracts. Custom objects let an account define its own record types, with fields and a name the company chooses. Every custom object needs a list view where people find, review and update its records. The deal list view is the most developed list today, so this brief starts from it and generalizes it. It takes the 42 deal capabilities and turns them into 37 general ones, 31 for regular users and 6 for admins, 5 of them new for custom objects. Seven deal-only capabilities, such as the pipeline filter and sequences, are left out because they depend on deal concepts. The example object throughout is Cars, with records like a Honda Civic and a Ford Focus. Admins can do everything a regular user can, plus bulk archive and delete, select beyond the loaded page, and restore deleted records. What anyone can do is always subject to their permissions on the object and to the features their company has. The Stories section lists every capability, and the two mockups let you try the general ones on the Cars list.

Context

The deal list view has 42 capabilities (what a user can see or do). This section lists them next to the general capabilities derived from them for custom objects. Example object: Cars, with Honda Civic 123 ABC and Ford Focus 456 DEF as records. Decisions and open questions
  • Regular vs admin. Deal rows with persona Rep or Both are regular. Rows with persona Manager are admin: select all beyond the page, bulk archive, bulk delete, bulk unarchive and the archiving automation banner. Admins can also do everything a regular user can.
  • Left out as deal-only: pipeline filter, sequences, convert to leads, contact enrichment, the deal-added automation banner, the participants coachmark, the new-account onboarding banner, and the totals and weighted value in the summary bar.
  • Quick filter chips keep the idea but use a field of the object (here Status) instead of won, lost and open.
  • Several deal stories merge: create and edit filter become one "Build a filter with conditions" story, archived list and archive view become one, and bulk progress and bulk error become one.
  • New for custom objects: R15 Bulk edit, R29 Import records, R30 Hover cards, R31 Show on map, A6 Restore deleted records.
  • Column layout is saved per filter, exactly as on the deal list: switching filter switches the columns, order and widths.
  • Everything is subject to the user's permissions on the object (edit, archive, delete, export) and to the company having the feature (data cleanup, automations, email).

Open questions

  • Who can restore deleted records: admins only (as drawn here) or anyone with delete permission?
  • Show on map needs an address field on the object. Does it appear only when one exists?
  • Send group email and Create activities assume a link to a contact. What happens for objects with no contact link?
  • Is the archive cap per object or per company?
